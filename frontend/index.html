How Inference Works

The Amazon TGAT evaluates the probability of a review occurring between a given User and Product at a specific Time.

  • Fetches the historical subgraph for the User and Product strictly before t_target.
  • Applies temporal attention with exponential decay (τ).
  • Computes the dot product of the contextual embeddings.
  • A low similarity score implies a highly anomalous interaction (e.g., review bot, hijacked account).
Note: Demo mode utilizes cached proxy embeddings for speed. Full production deployments require a graph database to query ego-networks in real-time.
