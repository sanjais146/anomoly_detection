# Final Scientific Claims

### Claims We Can Make
- The final model achieved 56.48% Test F1.
- It improved the clean XGBoost baseline by 6.79 percentage points.
- Causal behavioral features were highly valuable.
- T-GAT did not improve the strongest tabular model.
- Delayed label availability materially affects performance.
- Strict chronological evaluation produces substantially different results from the earlier 0-day-label experiment.

### Claims We Cannot Make
- We did not beat the 73% GNN-EADD benchmark.
- We cannot definitively prove GNN-EADD used leakage.
- We cannot claim T-GAT is universally inferior.
- We cannot claim 56.48% is the theoretical maximum possible on IEEE-CIS.
- We cannot claim the benchmark is invalid without reproducing its exact implementation.
