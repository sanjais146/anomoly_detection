Why Temporal?

E-commerce anomalies (like review bombing or account takeovers) are inherently temporal.

Static graphs treat a review from 2012 the same as a review from 10 minutes ago. TGAT utilizes a continuous-time decay function:

w = exp(-τ * Δt_days)

This forces the model to dynamically prioritize recent topological neighborhood structures, accurately mirroring the rapidly evolving nature of e-commerce fraud.

Different Datasets and Protocols
The Amazon TGAT and IEEE-CIS E10 models evaluate completely different datasets and anomaly definitions. They are presented together for completeness, NOT as a direct benchmark.

  • No Synthetic Entities: No fabricated users or products exist in the graph.

Self-Supervised Anomaly Definition

Because the raw dataset lacks explicit "fraud" tags, anomalies are defined topologically. We utilize Contrastive Negative Sampling:

Negative Edges (Anomalies): The model dynamically queries unobserved edges (a real user interacting with a real product they have zero genuine affinity/history with) to represent anomalous interactions (e.g., review bombing, account hijacking).

This strictly forces the TGAT to distinguish genuine topological behavior from random or malicious structural injections.
