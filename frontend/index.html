Decision Framework: How TGAT Settles on Genuine vs. Anomaly

Comprehensive mathematical breakdown of link reconstruction, temporal decay, and calibration

IEEE Methodology
STEP 1

Causal Temporal Aggregation

Neighbors aggregated with continuous-time decay:

w(Δt) = exp(-0.431 · Δt)

Strict causal masking guarantees no future timestamps contaminate the node representations.

STEP 2

16D Latent Projection

Buyer u and Product p are projected through multi-head graph attention:

h = LeakyReLU(W · [u || p || Δt])

Captures high-order co-purchasing, rating behavior, and category clustering.

STEP 3

Link Reconstruction Score

Inner product link probability is inverted to quantify surprise:

Score = 1.0 - σ(uᵀ · p)

Aligned vectors (uᵀp > 0) yield low score. Orthogonal / opposing vectors yield high score.

STEP 4

Decision Calibration (0.6668)

Decision boundary calibrated from empirical cold-start distribution:

Verdict = Score ≥ 0.6668 ? ANOMALY : GENUINE

Separates genuine cross-product affinity from fake reviews and topological anomalies.

Feature / Driver Genuine Interaction Profile (Score < 0.667) Anomalous Interaction Profile (Score ≥ 0.667)
Latent Cosine Similarity Positive alignment (> 0.0): The buyer's historical preference matches the target product cluster (e.g. camera buyer buying SD cards). Near-zero or negative (< 0.0): Topological divergence. User interacts with completely disconnected product space with zero historical correlation.
Rating Distribution Consistent (3★ – 5★): Aligns with the product's catalog average (4.1★ – 4.8★) with normal review variance. Rating Inversion Shock (1★ or Extreme 5★ burst): Severe deviation from product baseline, triggering rating disparity penalties.
Temporal Spacing (Δt) Organic cadence (> 2-7 days): Continuous-time decay factor w(Δt) reflects natural human purchasing behavior. Sub-minute or concentrated bursts (< 60s): Synchronized review campaigns that bypass organic decay rates, signaling bot activity.
Graph Bipartite Degree Well-connected hub / bridge: User or product has established edges in the bipartite Amazon graph. Isolated cold node / phantom bridge: Sudden high-volume connectivity between disjoint clusters with no common neighbors.

Why Temporal?

E-commerce anomalies (like review bombing or account takeovers) are inherently temporal.

Static graphs treat a review from 2012 the same as a review from 10 minutes ago. TGAT utilizes a continuous-time decay function:

w = exp(-τ * Δt_days)

This forces the model to dynamically prioritize recent topological neighborhood structures, accurately mirroring the rapidly evolving nature of e-commerce relationships and anomalies.

Self-Supervised Anomaly Detection

The Amazon Electronics review dataset does not provide explicit fraud labels for these interactions. The TGAT pipeline therefore uses contrastive negative sampling during self-supervised learning. Unobserved user–product relationships are used as negative samples so the model learns temporal and structural patterns of observed interactions.

Scientific Definition: Anomaly = temporally or structurally unexpected user–product interaction.
IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION: An anomaly score represents how unusual an interaction is relative to the learned temporal/structural graph patterns. A high anomaly score does NOT mean confirmed fraud.
